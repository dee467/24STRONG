About FND

What is Functional Neurological Disorder?

Functional Neurological Disorder (FND) is a condition where the nervous system doesn't work correctly, despite no structural damage — think of it like a software problem in an otherwise intact system.

FND can cause paralysis, seizures, blindness, loss of speech, and severe chronic pain. It is often invisible, deeply misunderstood, and notoriously difficult to diagnose. Despite affecting as many people as Multiple Sclerosis, it remains significantly under-researched. Many sufferers — like Megan — spend years searching for answers.

Symptoms include
→ Paralysis or weakness → Seizures → Loss of vision or speech → Chronic pain → Fatigue
The reality

Affects 1 in 3,000 people yet receives a fraction of the research funding of comparable neurological conditions.

Fundraising for Megan

This is Megan Dixon — 21 years old, a treasured part of our community family, and the reason every single session of 24 STRONG exists.

Megan suffers from severe FND (Functional Neurological Disorder). She has been fighting for her health since she was just 13 years old — facing paralysis, blindness, and years of extreme pain with extraordinary courage. After years of searching, she has finally found a surgeon willing to take on her case. Her only remaining surgical option is to have both legs amputated above the knee. Her surgery is scheduled for 20th August.

A specialist wheelchair — ideally electric — would give Megan back something she hasn't had since she was 13: independence. The ability to move freely without always relying on someone else would truly change her life.

"I just want to be normal, to have a normal life. If I manage to get an electric wheelchair, I will be able to regain more independence than I ever thought I'd have again." — Megan

Participation & Safety

👨‍👩‍👧 Children & Young People

Children under 16 are warmly welcome at 24 STRONG and must be accompanied by a responsible adult at all times during the event. Adults are responsible for the welfare and supervision of any child or young person in their care throughout all sessions.

⚠️ Participation Disclaimer

24 STRONG is a physical event involving strenuous exercise. All participants take part entirely at their own risk. By purchasing a ticket, you confirm that you are in suitable physical health to participate in physical activity and that you have sought appropriate medical advice if you have any existing health conditions or concerns. Neither Commando Boot Camp, Chipping Sodbury Rugby Club, nor any associated personal trainers accept liability for injury, illness, or loss sustained during the event. Participants are responsible for assessing their own fitness and ability before and during participation. If in doubt, please consult your GP before attending.

📍 Venue Note

Midnight to dawn sessions are held at our event partner's gym, Wickwar Strength & Fitness. All other sessions take place at Chipping Sodbury Rugby Club. Details of session locations are clearly marked on the timetable.
