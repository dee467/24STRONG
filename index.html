The Event

What is
24 Strong?

24 STRONG is a 24-hour relay bootcamp — 24 personal trainers, each leading one power-packed hour-long session back to back. From 5PM on Friday 3rd July through to 5PM on Saturday 4th July, the sessions never stop and neither do we.

Whether you're here for one session, a few hours, or the full 24-hour challenge — all fitness levels are welcome. Join for a drop-in, go the distance, or bring the whole family.

Overnight sessions (midnight through to dawn) move offsite to Wickwar Strength & Fitness — a purpose-built gym keeping the energy going through the witching hours.

Fundraising for Megan

This is Megan Dixon — 21 years old, a treasured part of our community family, and the reason every single session of 24 STRONG exists.

Megan suffers from severe FND (Functional Neurological Disorder). She has been fighting for her health since she was just 13 years old — facing paralysis, blindness, and years of extreme pain with extraordinary courage. After years of searching, she has finally found a surgeon willing to take on her case. Her only remaining surgical option is to have both legs amputated above the knee. Her surgery is scheduled for 20th August.

A specialist wheelchair — ideally electric — would give Megan back something she hasn't had since she was 13: independence. The ability to move freely without always relying on someone else would truly change her life.

"I just want to be normal, to have a normal life. If I manage to get an electric wheelchair, I will be able to regain more independence than I ever thought I'd have again." — Megan
